# Signsprout XR — Tripothon S1

Direction 03: VR / XR / AR. Solo entrant: Shivam Gupta. No Tool Track claimed.

## A learning garden, given to a family

Signsprout XR is a seated WebXR practice world for hearing families learning
their first ASL signs. A friendly seedling demonstrates each sign, glowing guide
hands help the learner follow, and feedback explains handshape, position and
movement. Each completed sign grows a plant in a garden that reflects practice
and review. It is a gift of a patient place to begin communicating together.

## How to try

On desktop, select “Watch it in your browser” for the simulated lesson. Explore
the 3D dictionary and sample family dashboard. On a Quest with hand tracking,
open the immersive app and select Start learning. Physical headset validation
has not been performed for this edition.

## Technology and limits

TypeScript, WebXR Hand Input, IWSDK, Three.js, pmndrs/uikit, React and Vite.
The public preview is local-first; accounts, cloud pairing and sync need a
separately configured Supabase backend. Demo hands are simulated, not physical
headset capture. The checker has synthetic tests, not clinical or real-user
validation. No Deaf signer has reviewed the vocabulary yet. Signsprout is a
practice aid, not a translator or replacement for Deaf teachers.
